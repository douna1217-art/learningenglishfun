Privacy Policy隐私政策

learningenglishfun is a reading site for children, used together with a parent or guardian. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it is stored, and how a parent can control it.

learningenglishfun 是一个供孩子在家长或监护人陪同下使用的阅读网站。本政策说明我们收集什么、为什么收集、存放在哪里，以及家长如何管理。

1. Who we are1. 我们是谁

Operator: [operator name / 运营者名称]. Contact: [contact email / 联系邮箱].

2. What we collect2. 我们收集什么

  • Parent account: your email address, and a record that you accepted these terms (the time and the version).
    家长账号：邮箱地址，以及你同意条款的记录（时间和版本）。
  • Child profile (up to 2 per parent): a nickname, a picture (one of a few animals) and, if you choose, a grade. We ask you not to use a real name.
    孩子档案（每位家长最多 2 个）：昵称、头像（几种小动物之一），以及你选填的年级。请不要使用真实姓名。
  • Learning activity of each child: books opened and finished, quiz answers, stars, the virtual pet and its items, Smart Review records and the reading-level test result.
    每个孩子的学习记录：打开和读完的书、答题情况、星星、虚拟宠物及其物品、智能复习记录、阅读水平测评结果。
  • Anonymous usage statistics: page visits, which books are opened, a random ID stored in the browser, the device time zone, browser language and a coarse device type (phone, tablet, computer or WeChat). We do not store IP addresses in our own statistics.
    匿名使用统计：访问次数、打开了哪些书、浏览器里随机生成的编号、设备时区、浏览器语言和大致的设备类型（手机、平板、电脑或微信）。我们自己的统计不保存 IP 地址。
  • Feedback you send (the message and, if you give it, an email address).
    你发送的反馈（内容，以及你自愿提供的邮箱）。

We do not collect: real names, birthdays, phone numbers, home addresses, precise location, photos, voice recordings or advertising identifiers.
我们不收集：真实姓名、生日、手机号、家庭住址、精确位置、照片、录音、广告标识。

3. Why we use it3. 用途

To run the service: sign you in, keep each child's progress and pet, let you continue on another device, recommend books and understand which books are useful. We do not show advertising and we do not sell personal information.
用于提供服务：登录、保存每个孩子的进度和宠物、换设备继续、推荐书籍，以及了解哪些书有用。我们不投放广告，也不出售个人信息。

4. Where it is stored4. 存放在哪里

Accounts and progress are stored with Supabase on servers in the United States. The website is delivered through Tencent Cloud, audio and images are stored on Tencent Cloud in Hong Kong, login emails are sent through Resend, and DNS is handled by Cloudflare. If you use the site from mainland China, your and your child's information is therefore stored outside mainland China, which you confirm when you create the account.
账号和进度通过 Supabase 存放在美国的服务器上。网站通过腾讯云分发，音频和图片存放在腾讯云香港，登录邮件由 Resend 发送，域名解析由 Cloudflare 提供。因此，如果你在中国大陆使用本网站，你和孩子的信息会存放在中国大陆以外，你在创建账号时已确认这一点。

5. Sharing5. 共享

Only with the service providers above, only to run the service. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
仅在提供服务所必需时，与上述服务商共享。不会提供给广告商或数据经纪商。

6. Your control as a parent6. 家长的权利

  • Delete a child and all of that child's progress at any time in the Parent area.
    随时可以在“家长区”删除某个孩子及其全部进度。
  • To see, correct or delete your account, or to withdraw your consent, write to [contact email / 联系邮箱].
    要查看、更正或删除账号，或撤回同意，请联系上面的邮箱。
  • The site also works without an account; progress is then kept only in your browser.
    不注册也可以使用，进度只保存在你的浏览器里。

7. Children7. 儿童

Accounts are created and controlled by a parent or guardian, who consents on the child's behalf. We keep the minimum needed for a child to learn.
账号由家长或监护人创建和管理，并代孩子表示同意。我们只保存孩子学习所需的最少信息。

8. Security and retention8. 安全与保存期限

Connections use HTTPS and every account can only read its own data. We keep data while the account exists; deleted children are removed from our database straight away. [retention for the whole account / 整个账号的保存期限]
连接使用 HTTPS，每个账号只能读取自己的数据。账号存在期间保存数据；被删除的孩子会立即从数据库中移除。

9. Changes9. 变更

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will ask you to agree again.
政策发生重要变化时，会再次请你确认。
