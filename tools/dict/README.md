# Word explanations for the books (work in progress)

- `headwords.json` – the base words of all the stories (made by `tools/dict_wordlist.py`): how often each is used, in how many books, the
  forms it takes (ran, running -> run) and the Chinese meanings from ECDICT.
- `names.txt` – what was taken for names of characters and left out (check it by eye); `unknown.txt` – words not in ECDICT.
- `samples.json` – 20 hand-written entries that show the planned style: a short English explanation a child can read, the Chinese meaning, one example
  sentence, up to two meanings for a word.

The Chinese meanings and base-word links come from **ECDICT** (https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT, MIT licence, copyright 2025 Linwei).
Keep that notice if the data is published with the site.
